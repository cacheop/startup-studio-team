# Customer problem

Use observed behavior and attributed statements to describe the problem.

## Customer and context

Describe who experiences the problem and the situation in which it occurs. Distinguish the user from the buyer.

## Current workflow and workaround

Explain how the customer handles the problem today and what that approach costs in time, money, or effort.

## Frequency and consequence

State how often the problem occurs and its consequences. Mark estimates as assumptions and cite observed measurements.

## Evidence and uncertainty

Link the research supporting these claims and identify what the team still needs to learn.
