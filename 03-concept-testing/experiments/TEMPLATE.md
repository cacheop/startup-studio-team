# Experiment

Copy this form using the [experiment guide](README.md). Change the record type to `experiment` in the copy.

## Hypothesis and decision

State the claim under test and the decision its result could change.

## Method and sample

Describe the procedure, target participants, sample rationale, conditions, and source of observations.

## Metric and success criterion

Define the metric, unit, denominator, and threshold before the test. Explain the result that would challenge the hypothesis.

## Test materials

Link the materials and identify their version when relevant.

## Observed result

The team has not recorded a result. After the test, state the actual sample, measurements, dates, and evidence links.

## Limitations and changes

Record deviations from the plan, inconclusive results, and limits on interpretation.

## Decision and next action

Explain what the result supports or challenges and what the team will do next. Include an owner and target date.
