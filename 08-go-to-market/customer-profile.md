# Initial buyer

Describe how the initial customer decides to buy.

## Buyer and purchase trigger

Identify the buyer's role, the event that creates demand, and the [market segment](../05-market/segments.md).

## Buying process

Describe decision makers, budget, procurement requirements, and the expected purchase timeline.

## Objections and evidence

Record objections and alternatives with links to customer observations. Separate assumptions from observed buying behavior.
