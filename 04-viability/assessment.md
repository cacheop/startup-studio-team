# Venture assessment

Evaluate whether the available evidence supports pursuing the venture.

## Demand

Link evidence that the intended customer experiences the problem and would act to solve it.

## Technical and operational feasibility

Explain what the team has demonstrated and what remains uncertain. Address constraints that affect delivery.

## Economics and customer access

Link the business model and acquisition evidence. State the assumptions that could change the conclusion.

## Current recommendation

Record whether to pursue, change direction, pause, or stop, with the reasons and next decision point. Link the dated decision record.
