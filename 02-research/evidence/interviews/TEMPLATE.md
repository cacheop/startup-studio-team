# Interview record

Copy this form using the [interview guide](README.md). Change the record type to `interview` in the copy.

## Participant context

Describe the participant's role, relevant experience, and relationship to the customer group under study.

## Questions and method

Record the questions, interview format, recruitment approach, and relevant conditions.

## Observations and quotations

Attribute quotations and distinguish observed behavior from what the participant reported.

## Interpretation and limitations

Explain what the team learned, what challenges its assumptions, and how far the findings extend.

## Source and next action

Link original notes or recordings when available. Identify the next question or action and its owner.
