# Published source record

Copy this form using the [source guide](README.md). Change the record type to `source` in the copy.

## Source identity

Record the publisher, title, author when available, original URL, and any saved source file.

## Relevant findings

State the specific claims or data the team uses. Include page numbers or sections where possible.

## Method and coverage

Explain the source's population, geography, period, method, and limits when relevant.

## Implication for the venture

Separate the source's findings from the team's interpretation. Link the analysis or decision that uses this source.
