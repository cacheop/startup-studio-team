# Survey record

Copy this form using the [survey guide](README.md). Change the record type to `survey` in the copy.

## Question and recruitment

State the research question, intended respondents, recruitment method, and collection conditions.

## Questionnaire and response data

Link the questionnaire and original response export. Record the number invited when known, responses received, and exclusions.

## Analysis and findings

Explain the analysis and report denominators alongside results. Distinguish observations from interpretation.

## Limitations and next action

Describe coverage limits and possible selection effects. Identify the next decision or test the findings inform.
